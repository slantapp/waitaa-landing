# **RINGA – Smart Calling Without Hardware**

## **Overview**

Ringa eliminates the need for physical call bells and manual signaling systems. Customers simply scan a QR code and instantly notify staff.

## **How It Works**

* Customer scans QR code at table or location  
* Sends request or message instantly  
* Staff receives real-time notification with exact table/location  
* Staff responds and can send feedback back to the customer

---

# **MENU – Smart Ordering Without Waiting**

## **Overview**

Menu allows customers to browse and order directly from their phones — no need to call a waiter.

---

## **How It Works**

* Customer scans QR code  
* Views digital menu  
* Selects items and places order  
* Business receives order instantly  
* Staff processes and updates order status

### **2\. Real-Time Order Management**
